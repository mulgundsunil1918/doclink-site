The Problem

Over a million doctors.
No digital presence.

India's independent practitioners are running entire practices on tools that were never designed for medicine. It's time that changed.

1M+
independent doctors
in India

Most solo practitioners in India have no website, no booking page, no digital identity outside their local area. They are invisible to patients beyond walking distance, and they have no way to grow beyond word-of-mouth referrals.

Unlike salaried hospital doctors, independent physicians own their practice — but they're running it with the wrong tools. WhatsApp for messages. Google Forms for appointments. Handwritten slips for prescriptions. Cash for payments. No records. No analytics. No system.

Doclink replaces all of that with a single, premium, compliant platform the doctor owns and brands as their own — without needing a hospital or a marketplace like Practo behind them.

How It Works

From signup to first
consultation in minutes.

No technical setup. No IT team. Three steps and your digital clinic is live.

1
Sign Up & Get Your Clinic

Create your Doclink account in under 2 minutes. You immediately get a branded profile page with your name, specialty, and photo — plus a unique QR code and shareable WhatsApp link.

🌐 Unique profile URL 📱 QR code generated instantly 💬 WhatsApp booking link

Patients Book in 60 Seconds

Patients scan your QR code at your clinic, tap your WhatsApp link, or find you online. They select a slot, confirm — and they're booked. No app download required for patients.

📷 Scan QR → booked 🔗 WhatsApp link booking ⏱️ Under 60 seconds

Consult, Prescribe, Get Paid

Run the consultation by video, audio, or chat. Write a digital branded prescription that the patient receives instantly. Collect payment via UPI — all within Doclink. Everything is recorded automatically.

Features

Everything your practice needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Eight tools, one platform. Each built to replace something you're currently doing the hard way.

🌐
Branded Doctor Profile

Your own digital identity. A professional profile page with your name, photo, specialty, fees, and booking — under your brand, not Practo's.

📱
QR Code + WhatsApp Booking

Print your QR on your prescription pad, visiting card, or clinic board. Patients scan and book in under 60 seconds. Or share your WhatsApp link anywhere.

🎥
Video / Audio / Chat Consults

Run consultations the way that suits the patient — HD video for complex cases, audio for quick follow-ups, chat for reports and queries. All encrypted.

💳
Integrated UPI Payments

Collect consultation fees via UPI, directly inside the app. Automatic receipts, revenue dashboard, and zero cash handling. Revenue scales with your earnings.

📋
Digital Branded Prescriptions

Write prescriptions digitally with your name and registration number. Patients receive a PDF instantly. Tamper-proof, traceable, and pharmacist-ready.

📊
Patient History & Records

Every consultation, prescription, note, and payment — automatically logged to the patient's record. No more paper files or scattered WhatsApp histories.

🤖
AI Clinical Assistant

Generate prescription drafts, SOAP notes, and drug interaction checks in seconds. Powered by Groq's Llama 3.3-70B with clinical prompts built for Indian practice.

📈
Practice Analytics

See your busiest days, top patient conditions, monthly revenue, and consultation trends — all in one dashboard. Know your practice like never before.

About

Built by a doctor
who ran out of excuses.

The tools existed. The problem was obvious. The million independent practitioners running their practices on workarounds wasn't going to fix itself.

Doclink is a doctor-first platform — meaning every feature was designed from the perspective of the physician, not the platform. You own your patient relationships. You own your data. You keep your brand. Doclink just gives you the infrastructure.

It runs on a simple model: free to start, transaction-fee on consultations, optional SaaS subscription for advanced features. Revenue scales directly with yours. If you earn more, we grow with you.
