What Holds Healthcare Back

Every doctor knows these problems.
Nobody solved them — until now.

These aren't hypothetical. They're the daily reality of practicing medicine in India.

⏱️
Pain Point
40% Paperwork

Doctors spend nearly half their time writing repeat prescriptions, chasing lab reports, and filling forms — instead of treating patients.

🌍
Pain Point
Access Crisis

70% of India's specialist doctors are in just 6 cities. Patients in rural Karnataka, Bihar, or the North-East have no access to specialist care.

💊
Pain Point
Prescription Fraud

Paper prescriptions are forged, duplicated, and used to obtain controlled substances. No accountability. No traceability.

📁
Pain Point
Lost Records

Patients carry crumpled paper files that get lost, damaged, or forgotten. Every new doctor starts from scratch — repeating tests, missing critical history.

💬
Pain Point
Unsecured Comms

Doctors share patient X-rays and reports over WhatsApp. Medical consultations happen in unencrypted chats. No HIPAA-aligned channel exists.

💵
Pain Point
Invisible Billing

Most clinics run on cash with no analytics. Doctors don't know their peak hours, revenue trends, or most-visited patients. Growth is invisible.

The Solution

Doclink bridges the gap.

One unified digital layer for Indian healthcare — putting the right tools in every doctor's hands, for free, forever.

  • AI-generated prescriptions, SOAP notes, and drug info in seconds — not hours
  • QR-coded digital Rx that patients carry on their phones, tamper-proof and traceable
  • Encrypted telemedicine consultations — no WhatsApp needed
  • Complete patient records accessible in one tap, anywhere in India
  • Practice analytics: appointments, revenue, retention — all in one dashboard
  • Family health management — full history for every household member
  • Integrated Razorpay payments for transparent, digital billing

Features

🤖
AI Clinical Assistant

Prescription drafts, SOAP notes, drug interaction checks, and clinical Q&A — powered by Groq's Llama 3.3-70B with clinical system prompts.

📋
Digital Prescriptions

QR-coded Rx that patients carry forever on their phones. Tamper-proof, traceable, and shareable with any pharmacy or specialist.

🎥
Telemedicine

HD video consultations from anywhere. Encrypted end-to-end. No third-party apps. Just open Doclink and connect.

📊
Patient Records

Complete medical history — diagnoses, prescriptions, notes, allergies — in one searchable, encrypted record. Always up to date.

💊
Drug Interaction Checker

AI-powered interaction screening for any drug combination. Severity ratings (safe / caution / major) with actionable clinical guidance.

📈
Practice Analytics

Dr. Sunil Mulgund

Every problem Doclink solves is one I encountered in my own practice. Paper records that vanished. Prescriptions that got forged. Colleagues sharing patient scans over WhatsApp because there was nothing better. I built Doclink — and the broader Bridgr ecosystem — because the gap is real, and someone had to close it.

Visit bridgr.co.in

Doclink is part of the
Bridgr ecosystem.

Bridgr is a suite of free clinical tools — each one starting as a gap noticed in the ward or the clinic. Medshelf, Wardly, Pediaid, CureDesk, DocShelf — and now Doclink.

The philosophy is simple: every doctor in India, regardless of their location or resources, should have access to the same quality tools as the best-equipped urban hospitals. Free. Forever. No paywalls, no ads.
